About this page

LittleBuggy is an information page for families — we do not run programs and cannot register your child or guarantee a spot. Dates and rules come from the official city websites, checked November 22, 2026; cities do change dates (Richmond's winter registration night was postponed this season), so confirm in the official system before your registration day. The universal rules still apply: create the account and add your children days early, know the level or category you need, and join the waitlist the moment a class shows full.
