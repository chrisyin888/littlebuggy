Before registration day

  • Create the account days early. Some cities take days to approve new accounts or verify resident status.
  • Add every child to the account, with birthdays — age and level checks happen at checkout.
  • Know your child's level — most cities require the previous level on file; other cities' report cards can usually be submitted ahead of time.
  • Use wish lists / favourites where the system offers them.
  • Residents usually register first — check your city's gap below.
  • Join the waitlist immediately if your class is full — emailed offers can expire in 24–36 hours.

Surrey

Next registration: Residents — most programs: Sun, Nov 29, 2026 at 9:00 pm. Residents — aquatic programs (swim lessons): Sun, Dec 13, 2026 at 9:00 pm. Non-residents — all programs: Sun, Dec 20, 2026 at 9:00 pm.

Pools: Surrey Sport & Leisure Complex (Fleetwood) · Guildford Recreation Centre · Grandview Heights Aquatic Centre · South Surrey Indoor Pool · Newton Recreation Centre

Eight outdoor pools run in summer only.

  • Create your MySurrey account before registration day. Surrey residents must verify their address at any recreation facility front desk (bring government ID with address) and keep a current photo on file for each family member.
  • Virtual waiting room: log in between 8:45–8:59 pm on registration night — spots are assigned randomly at 9:00 pm, and logging in earlier gives no advantage. After 9:00 pm it is first come, first served.
  • Waitlists are by email: if a spot opens you get 24 hours to confirm and pay, or the spot goes to the next person.
  • Free starter lessons: Surrey residents ages 3–12 pay $0 for entry-level preschool and Swimmer 1–3 lessons (shown automatically at registration). Withdrawing within 7 days of the start date costs a $25 fee.
  • Phone registration: 604-501-5100 (Mon–Fri, 8:30 am–4:30 pm). The city recommends Chrome.

Pools: Britannia · Hillcrest · Kensington · Kerrisdale · Killarney · Lord Byng · Renfrew · Templeton · Vancouver Aquatic Centre
